PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

This is a demonstration website. AEROSTRIDE is a fictional brand and no real products are sold. The policy below describes how this demo handles information.

Information we store

When you place a demo order, subscribe to the newsletter or send a message, we store what you enter (such as name, email, phone number, shipping address, and message) in the site's database so the feature can work.

Information we do not collect

We never ask for or store payment card details. No payments are processed.

Browser storage

Your bag and wishlist are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. A strictly necessary session cookie protects forms against forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can clear your bag and wishlist at any time by clearing site data in your browser. To request removal of information you submitted, contact support@aerostride.example.
